# NovaDrive CRO Decision Support Platform

An interactive decision-support web application designed for Chief Risk Officers (CRO) and supply chain risk leadership to visualize, evaluate, and mitigate multi-tier operational and environmental risks.

---

## 🎯 Purpose: Why We Are Building This

Modern automotive and technology supply chains are vulnerable to multi-tier disruption—ranging from semiconductor fab bottlenecks to climate hazards affecting critical component production.

The **NovaDrive CRO Decision Support Platform** bridges the gap between raw supply chain data and strategic risk leadership. We built this platform to:
* **Centralize Tier & Sub-Tier Visibility**: Track risk exposure across multi-tiered component suppliers (e.g., sub-tier die fabricators, PCB manufacturers, and assembly plants).
* **Quantify Financial Exposure**: Provide executive leadership with actionable dollar-impact metrics ($1B+ hazard exposures in flood zones, geographic risks, etc.).
* **Accelerate Mitigation Decision-Making**: Enable interactive scenario modeling, allowing risk officers to rapidly prioritize high-exposure vulnerabilities and allocate risk-reduction resources effectively.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Interactive Risk Dashboard**: Real-time filtering and visualization of key risk indicators across suppliers and component tiers.
* **Geographic & Environmental Hazard Tracking**: Map supplier facilities against environmental risks such as flood zones and localized disruption events.
* **Financial Exposure Analytics**: Quantify total dollar-at-risk for critical assemblies (bare PCBs, IC dies, sub-components).
* **Decision Support Workflows**: Streamlined interface designed for cross-functional collaboration between Risk, Procurement, and Operations teams.
